# Sample Outputs & Intelligence Previews

The GeM Procurement Intelligence Platform transforms raw, messy HTML into structured, analytics-ready intelligence. Below are examples of the data outputs generated by the platform.

## 1. Cleaned Vendor Intelligence (`cleaned_vendor_evaluations.csv`)

This dataset captures the competition dynamics within a single bid, computing the L1/L2 pricing spreads and assigning anomaly flags.

| bid_id | vendor_name | vendor_rank | quoted_price | technically_qualified | financially_qualified | awarded_flag | l1_price | l1_vs_vendor_pct | anomaly_abnormal_spread |
|--------|-------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|--------------|----------|------------------|-------------------------|
| GEM/2026/B/7537433 | EVERSHINE ENTERPRISES | L1 | 23482.00 | True | True | True | 23482.00 | 0.00 | False |
| GEM/2026/B/7537433 | FRIENDS ENTERPRISES | L2 | 25300.00 | True | True | False | 23482.00 | 7.74 | False |
| GEM/2026/B/7537433 | National Auto Spares | L3 | 27800.00 | True | True | False | 23482.00 | 18.39 | False |

*Insight: EVERSHINE ENTERPRISES won the contract with a 7.74% price gap below the L2 competitor.*

*Note: The 19 missing ministries are valid state-level departments (e.g., "Energy Department Uttar Pradesh") which do not have a federal Ministry parent.*
